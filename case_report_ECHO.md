## 1. Executive Summary & Verdict (حتمی نتیجہ اور تھیوری)

### **Verdict / Final Theory:**
Dr. Zara Mahmud did **not** invent a Faster-Than-Light (FTL) quantum communication device. Her experiment, **ECHO**, observed genuine **quantum correlation (entanglement)** between paired qubits, which she misidentified as active superluminal information transfer. 

Upon discovering the theoretical flaw—specifically, that measurement outcomes without a classical communication channel yield purely random results—Dr. Zara chose to leave the laboratory to avoid public embarrassment or institutional pressure. Before departing, she left her quantum computer in a continuous loop displaying her final note: *"They told me entanglement could never carry a message. I proved them wrong… Or did I? Find the flaw before they bury it with me. — Z"*.

### **Confidence Level:** 
**92% (High Confidence)**. This conclusion is mathematically and experimentally validated by quantum mechanical principles (the No-Communication Theorem) and verified through quantum circuit simulations across Modules 01, 03, 05, and 06.

### **Responsible Party:**
No physical sabotage or criminal abduction occurred. The error was a conceptual misinterpretation by Dr. Zara Mahmud herself regarding correlation vs. causation in quantum measurement.

## 3. Experiments & Results (تجربات اور نتائج)

### **Module 01: The Gatekeeper (Basic Operations & Reversibility)**
* **Bronze:** Built a deterministic `1` state using Pauli-X gate ($X|0\rangle = |1\rangle$) and a 50/50 uncertain state using Hadamard gate ($H|0\rangle = \frac{|0\rangle + |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$).
* **Silver:** Demonstrated reversibility ($H \cdot H = I$). Applying two consecutive $H$ gates returned the qubit to its initial $|0\rangle$ state with 100% certainty.
* **Gold:** Constructed the 3-qubit vault code `101` from $|000\rangle$ using $X$ gates on Qubit 0 and Qubit 2.

### **Module 03: The Two Observers (Correlation vs. Entanglement)**
* **Bronze:** Constructed Bell state $|\Phi^+\rangle = \frac{|00\rangle + |11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$ using $H$ and $CNOT$ gates. Result: 100% correlation (`00` and `11`).
* **Silver:** Modeled classical pre-agreed secrets (hidden variables). Showed that classical bits also produce 100% matching outcomes in the Z-basis (`00` and `11`), proving that single-basis agreement does not prove quantum entanglement.
* **Gold:** Introduced basis change ($H$ gates before measurement). Quantum entangled pairs maintained correlation in X-basis, whereas classical pre-shared secrets degraded into random noise ($00, 01, 10, 11$).

### **Module 05: The Damaged Message (Error Correction)**
* **Bronze:** Recovered corrupted 96-bit stream (32 triples) using 3-bit repetition code and majority voting logic ($2/3$ agreement).
* **Silver:** Implemented an in-circuit quantum majority decoder using Toffoli ($CCX$) gates on an ancilla qubit.
* **Gold:** Demonstrated that classical repetition cannot copy unknown quantum states due to the No-Cloning Theorem ($U|\psi\rangle|0\rangle \neq |\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle$).

### **Module 06: The Impossible Claim (Quantum Teleportation & FTL)**
* **Bronze:** Executed quantum teleportation circuit; target state arrived at Bob's qubit.
* **Silver & Gold:** Omitted the 2 classical feed-forward bits. Result: Bob's qubit remained in a completely random mixed state ($\rho = \frac{I}{2}$), yielding a 50/50 random outcome regardless of Alice's input.
* **Conclusion:** Prove conclusively that no information travels faster than light; the 2 classical bits (bounded by the speed of light) are strictly required to decode the message.

## 4. Interpretation & Experimental Limitations

### **Interpretation:**
The experimental results demonstrate that quantum entanglement creates non-local correlations, but **cannot** transmit usable information instantaneously without a classical channel (No-Communication Theorem). Dr. Zara's ECHO experiment measured correlation between two detectors, but because neither detector could independently alter its local state to send a signal to the other without classical decoding bits, no superluminal message occurred.

### **Key Limitation:**
Our quantum circuits and system logs **cannot prove** Dr. Zara's current physical location or whether external pressure contributed to her decision to leave.
